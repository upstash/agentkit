Support eve 1.0 (`eve@1.0.0-rc.9`).

- `@upstash/agentkit-eve-extension` is now built with eve 1.0.0-rc.9 and needs eve `>=1.0.0-rc.9`. On eve 0.91, stay on `@upstash/agentkit-eve-extension@0.19`.
- `@upstash/agentkit-eve` keeps its `eve` peer range (`>=0.87.0 || >=1.0.0-0`). `redisMemory()` tools now show readable activity labels ("Saving to memory", "Searching memory", ...) instead of the prefixed tool name.
