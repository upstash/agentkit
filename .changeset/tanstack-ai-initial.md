New package: production backends for TanStack AI on Upstash Redis. `upstashPersistence()` (messages, runs, interrupts and metadata for `withPersistence()`, passing TanStack's conformance suite), `upstashStream()` (resumable `StreamDurability` on Redis Streams), `upstashLocks()` (distributed `LockStore` for `withLocks()`), `upstashMemory()` (a `MemoryAdapter` for `memoryMiddleware()` ranked in Redis Search), `toolCache()` and `rateLimit()` chat middlewares, and `createSearchTools()`.
